Frequently Asked Questions

How can WebToolkitBox be 100% free with no limits?

Unlike traditional websites that process files on expensive cloud servers, WebToolkitBox uses modern client-side JavaScript and WebAssembly to process images directly inside your own browser. Since we don't have to pay for massive file hosting and cloud computation, we can pass 100% of the savings back to you and keep the tools unlimited.

Do my images get saved on any server?

No. Your images are never uploaded to our servers. All conversions, cropping, compression, and AI background removal tasks run locally on your device. Your privacy is fully secured.

How do I convert HEIC photos from my iPhone?

Simply upload your HEIC files inside the Image Converter tool, select your target output format (JPG, PNG, or WebP), and click download. You can upload dozens of HEIC files at once to convert them in batch.

Does the background remover support high-resolution output?

Yes. While other sites downscale your images to standard resolution on free tiers, WebToolkitBox preserves your original file dimensions and downloads your transparent cutouts at full resolution.
