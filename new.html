Frequently Asked Questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

expand_more

No. All processing happens locally in your browser using WebAssembly and WebGL. Your data never leaves your device, ensuring complete privacy and zero server latency.

What formats are supported for upscaling?

expand_more

We currently support JPEG, PNG, WebP, and HEIC. For best results, we recommend starting with the highest quality original you have available.

Is there a file size limit?

expand_more

Because processing happens locally, the limit is based on your device's available RAM. Generally, files up to 50MB process smoothly on modern hardware.
