How do I resize an image to 100x100 pixels?

Upload your photo, type '100' in Width and '100' in Height, choose your resize strategy (Fit, Stretch, or Fill), and click Apply Resize to download your 100x100 image instantly.

Can I resize photos in bulk to one custom size?

Yes! Click the "Bulk Batch" tab, drag and drop multiple photos or entire folders, set your target Width and Height, and click "Resize All Images in Bulk". You can download all converted photos in a single ZIP package.

Can I resize photos for US and Indian passport/exam applications?

Yes! Click the 1-click preset chips for US Passport (600x600 px) or Indian Govt Exam (350x450 px) to immediately set official dimensions.
