How to Convert Image Formats Online

WebToolkitBox makes it extremely easy to convert image files without losing quality. Follow these simple steps:

  1. Upload Images: Drag and drop your image files or click inside the upload box to select them from your device. You can upload multiple files at once for batch processing.
  2. Select Output Format: Choose the target format you want to convert to (PNG, JPG/JPEG, WebP, GIF, or BMP).
  3. Adjust Settings: Customize conversion settings such as the quality slider (for lossy formats like JPG/WebP) to optimize file sizes.
  4. Download Files: Click the download button for individual converted images, or download all converted images at once in a single ZIP package.
