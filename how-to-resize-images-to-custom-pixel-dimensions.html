1. Why Do You Need Custom Pixel Dimensions?

Whether you are preparing a profile picture (e.g. 150x150px), creating a custom website favicon (e.g. 32x32px), or filling out a government application form requiring specific passport photograph dimensions (e.g. 300x400px), resizing images to exact pixel configurations is a common requirement.

2. Understanding Aspect Ratio Lock

When resizing, keeping the aspect ratio locked preserves the original proportions of the image so it doesn't look stretched or squeezed. If you need to fit it into a different ratio, you can select strategies like Contain (fits inside the box) or Cover (fills the box and crops extra parts).

3. How to Resize Image Pixels Online

  1. Navigate to the Image Dimension Resizer.
  2. Upload your photo. The tool will display the original size.
  3. Input your target width or height in the pixel boxes. Toggle the lock button to lock or unlock proportions.
  4. Choose your scaling strategy: Stretch, Contain, or Cover.
  5. Select output format (PNG, JPG, WebP) and click Apply Resize to download.

4. Browser-Based Resizing: Safety First

Resizing images with WebToolkitBox runs client-side. Your original photos are processed locally on your computer, protecting your files from third-party server analysis.
