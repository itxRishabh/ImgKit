Why is Lanczos3 sampling superior to browser scaling?

Standard browser canvas scaling uses basic bilinear blurring. The Pica WASM engine utilizes the mathematical Lanczos3 windowed sinc filter with WebWorker multi-threading to reconstruct missing high-frequency details with sharp clarity.
