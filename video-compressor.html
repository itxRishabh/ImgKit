Frequently Asked Questions

How can I compress videos without losing quality?

Use our Compress Video tool. Select target quality and resolution scale, and download. It uses libx264 encoding to reduce video file sizes while retaining visual sharpness.

Why is video compression secure?

For small videos (<20MB), conversion runs locally in-browser. For larger videos, files are securely processed on our secure cloud serverless pipeline and deleted immediately.
