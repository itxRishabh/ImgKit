Compress Video Files Locally (MP4 & WebM Converter)

Reduce the size of video files inside your browser using hardware-accelerated local compilation:

  1. Upload Video: Import your MP4, MOV, or WebM video file.
  2. Choose Target Quality: Adjust resolution and bitrate percentages.
  3. Run WASM Compression: The browser loads a local WASM FFmpeg compiler to re-encode the video.
  4. Download: Save your compressed video.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I compress videos without losing quality?

Use our Compress Video tool. Select target quality and resolution scale, and download. It uses libx264 encoding to reduce video file sizes while retaining visual sharpness.

Why is video compression secure?

For small videos (<20MB), conversion runs locally in-browser. For larger videos, files are securely processed on our secure cloud serverless pipeline and deleted immediately.
