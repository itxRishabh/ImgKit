Frequently Asked Questions

How does image trimming work?

The tool scans PNG pixel opacity data, finds the edges of the visible subject, and crops away empty transparent margins instantly.

Will it work on JPG files?

No, JPEG files do not support transparency. Please use PNG or WebP files with alpha channels.
