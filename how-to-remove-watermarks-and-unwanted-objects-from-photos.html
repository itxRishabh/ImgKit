1. The Impact of Watermarks & distracting objects on Photos

Whether it's an auto-generated timestamp from your digital camera, a photobomber in a perfect vacation picture, or a distracting watermark, unwanted background objects can ruin the professional aesthetic of an image. Learning how to cleanly remove them is essential for creators, businesses, and casual photographers.

2. What is Telea Fast Marching Image Inpainting?

Rather than relying on third-party servers to upload and modify your private photos, modern web tools utilize advanced client-side algorithms.
