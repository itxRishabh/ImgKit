2. How to Prepare Your Signature for Upload

  1. Sign on Clean White Paper: Use a dark black or blue ballpoint/gel pen on unruled white paper.
  2. Take a Close-Up Photo: Hold your phone directly above the paper without casting a phone shadow.
  3. Crop to Signature Boundary: Crop excess margins around the signature so the text fills the canvas.
  4. Compress to 10-20 KB: Use the Size Manager tool, enter 20 KB target value, and export as JPG.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the photo size limit for SSC CGL / CHSL application forms?

SSC requires a scanned passport photo in JPEG format with a file size between 20 KB and 50 KB and image dimensions of 3.5 cm width by 4.5 cm height (about 350x450 pixels).

What is the signature file size for UPSC and government exams?

Signatures must be scanned in JPG format with a file size strictly between 10 KB and 20 KB (or up to 50 KB for UPSC) and dimensions around 3.5 cm width by 1.5 cm height (280x120 pixels).
