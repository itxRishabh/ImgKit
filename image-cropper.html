Crop Photos Online with Exact Aspect Ratios & Presets

Trim image borders and focus on key subjects using aspect ratio presets optimized for popular social networks:

  1. Upload Image: Select the image file you want to crop.
  2. Select Preset Ratios: Choose from standard ratios like 1:1 (square profiles), 16:9 (videos/screens), 4:3, 3:2, or 9:16 (vertical mobile stories).
  3. Adjust Grid selection: Drag the corner handles or move the selection grid over the desired focal point.
  4. Export: Select your preferred format (PNG, JPG, WebP) and download the cropped file.
