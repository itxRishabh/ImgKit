Frequently Asked Questions

How do I turn a phone photo of my signature into a transparent PNG?

Sign on plain white paper, take a photo with your phone, and upload it to WebToolkitBox Transparent Signature Maker. The tool automatically removes the paper background and shadow, turns the ink into pure black or blue, and outputs a transparent PNG.

How do I insert this transparent signature into a PDF document?

Download the transparent PNG signature. Open your PDF in Adobe Acrobat, Apple Preview, Google Docs, or Word, click 'Insert Image', and place the signature over the signature line. The transparent background will blend seamlessly with the document.

Is my signature safe and confidential?

Yes! WebToolkitBox processes your signature 100% locally inside your browser. Your signature is never uploaded to any server or database, ensuring total security and privacy.
