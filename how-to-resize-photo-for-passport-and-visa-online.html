1. Standard International Passport Photo Specifications

Applying for a passport or visa requires strict compliance with biometric photo guidelines established by the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO). Submitting an incorrect pixel dimension or file size is the #1 cause of online application rejection.

🇺🇸 United States Passport & Visa

  • Physical Dimension: 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm) square.
  • Digital Pixel Dimensions: Minimum 600 × 600 pixels, up to 1200 × 1200 pixels (300 DPI).
  • Head Size Ratio: The head must measure between 1 inch and 1 3/8 inches (25 mm to 35 mm) from the bottom of the chin to the top of the head.
  • Background: Pure plain white or off-white. No textures or shadows.
  • File Format: JPEG / JPG (≤ 240 KB).

🇮🇳 India Passport (Passport Seva & OCI)

  • Physical Dimension: 3.5 cm width × 4.5 cm height (35 × 45 mm).
  • Digital Pixel Dimensions: 413 × 531 pixels @ 300 DPI (or 350 × 450 px @ 200 DPI).
  • Background: Plain white background with 70% to 80% facial coverage.
  • File Size Limit: Maximum 200 KB in JPG/JPEG format.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom & 🇪🇺 Schengen Visa

  • Physical Dimension: 35 mm width × 45 mm height.
  • Digital Pixel Dimensions: 413 × 531 pixels minimum (up to 826 × 1062 pixels @ 600 DPI).
  • Background: Light grey or light cream (not pure white for UK).
  • File Size: Between 50 KB and 10 MB.

🇨🇦 Canada Passport & Permanent Residency

  • Physical Dimension: 50 mm width × 70 mm height (2 × 2 3/4 inches).
  • Digital Pixel Dimensions: 420 × 588 pixels @ 300 DPI.
  • Face Height: 31 mm to 36 mm from chin to crown.
  • Background: Plain white or light-coloured.

2. How to Take a Compliant Passport Photo with Your Smartphone

  1. Stand Against a Plain Wall: Stand 3-4 feet in front of a flat, solid white or light cream wall to prevent harsh cast shadows.
  2. Use Natural Light: Face a window with diffused daylight to ensure balanced lighting on both sides of your face. Never use a harsh camera flash.
  3. Camera Positioning: Have a friend hold the phone at eye level 4-6 feet away. Keep your shoulders square and look directly into the camera lens.
  4. Neutral Facial Expression: Keep both eyes open, mouth closed, and avoid smiling or tilting your head. Remove eyeglasses, headphones, or hats.

Can I wear glasses in my passport photo?

In the United States and most countries, eyeglasses are not permitted in passport photos unless you have a signed medical statement for recent eye surgery.
