What is a HEIC File and Why Can't Windows Open It?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is Apple's default photo format for iPhones running iOS 11 and later. While HEIC offers excellent compression, older Windows PCs, web browsers, and online application forms often reject `.heic` files.
