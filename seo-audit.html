Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why is technical SEO important for Google rankings?

Technical SEO ensures search engine spiders (like Googlebot) can efficiently crawl, render, and index your website content. Fixing broken canonicals, missing meta tags, and slow page speed directly increases organic search rankings and click-through rates (CTR).
